WARDEN-9

Vanguard Run

1–4 player run-and-gun. Pick a hero, grab capsules, carry two weapons, take down the Warden.

Press your Fire button to join. Left / Right changes hero. Enter or pad Start begins.

P1: WASD · J fire · K jump · L swap weapon · I hold position  |  P2: arrows · , fire · . jump · / swap · ; hold  |  Gamepads: stick/d-pad, A jump, X fire, B swap, RB hold
